Collaboration with **Prof. Gustavo Sandoval** at NYU Courant investigating why GPT-2 fails at comparing numbers such as 9.11 and 9.8.

- Applies **mechanistic interpretability** techniques including probing and attention-head analysis
- Aims to understand how heuristic encodings emerge in transformer representations
- Ongoing research project
