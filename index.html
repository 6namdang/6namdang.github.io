hnd@mit.edu

I think it was never about the ideas or achievements, but the people and the community. Having a strong team with good and kind people always win.

I’m a research assistant in the Kellis Lab at MIT CSAIL, where I work on MANTIS AI with Prof. Manolis Kellis. I lead the Mantis4Hospital integration and built MantisFlybase for single-cell RNA-seq.

I’m also a research scholar at the Spaulding Neuromodulation Center at Harvard Medical School, working with Kevin Pacheco-Barrios. There I built Valor and Integral, two platforms for longitudinal concussion research.

Most of what I build is software that helps researchers and clinicians work with hard data: clinical records, single-cell sequencing, and studies that follow patients for years.

Right now I’m learning more about computational genomics, clinical informatics, and agentic systems for biology. I studied computer science at Gannon University and live in Boston.
